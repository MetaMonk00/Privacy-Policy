1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to our website, our contact and quote forms, client portals, and the services we provide, including web design and development, software and app development, hosting, maintenance, and related support (together, the "Services").

It does not cover websites or applications we build for clients. Those are governed by each client's own privacy policy.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number, company name, and job title.
  • Project information: descriptions of your needs, budgets, timelines, files, brand assets, and content you send us.
  • Account and billing details: billing address and payment information. Card payments are handled by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Communications: messages, emails, meeting notes, and support tickets.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring pages, pages viewed, and time spent on our site.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: see Cookies & Analytics.

Information from third parties

We may receive information from referral partners, public business directories, or platforms you connect to our Services (for example, a code repository or hosting account you give us access to).

3. How We Use Information

  • Respond to inquiries and prepare proposals and estimates.
  • Design, build, deliver, host, and maintain your projects.
  • Send invoices, process payments, and manage accounts.
  • Provide customer support and project updates.
  • Improve our website, Services, and internal processes.
  • Send newsletters or marketing emails, if you opt in. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.
We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
Text messaging: Mobile numbers and text message consent are never shared or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our SMS Program for details.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep our site working, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use it. These fall into three groups:

  • Essential: needed for the site to function (for example, security and form submission).
  • Analytics: help us measure traffic and improve content (for example, Google Analytics or a similar tool).
  • Preferences: remember settings such as your cookie choices.

You can control cookies through your browser settings or our cookie banner, where available. Blocking some cookies may affect how the site works.

5. How We Share Information

We share personal information only as described below:

  • Service providers: vendors who help us run our business, such as hosting, cloud infrastructure, email, project management, analytics, and payment processing. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Subcontractors: vetted developers or designers who help with your project, bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal requirements: when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect our rights, users, or the public.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.
  • With your consent: in any other case where you have asked us to.

6. Client Project Data

When we build, host, or maintain a website or application for a client, we may access data belonging to that client's own customers or users (such as database records, form submissions, or user accounts).

In those cases we act as a service provider / data processor on the client's behalf. We use that data only to perform the work the client has requested, under the client's instructions and our agreement with them. Questions about that data should be directed to the client that controls it.

7. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, including to provide the Services, keep business and tax records, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Inquiry data from prospects who do not become clients is generally deleted within 24 months. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, multi-factor authentication on internal systems, and regular software updates. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your personal information.
  • Receive a copy of your information in a portable format.
  • Opt out of marketing communications.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Residents of California (CCPA/CPRA), the EU/UK (GDPR), and other jurisdictions with privacy laws may exercise these rights by contacting us using the details below. We will verify your request and respond within the time required by law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where required). If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. International Transfers

We are based in the United States, and our service providers may process data in other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, for transfers of personal information outside your country.

12. Third-Party Links

Our site and portfolio may link to websites we do not control, including sites we have built for clients. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to read their policies.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we may notify you by email or a notice on our site.
